# Pair-lock v2: conditional dynamic hedge with verified matched-pair accounting (2026-10-02)

Paper only. The bot is `tpb-pairlock-paper` (`ecosystem.pairlock.config.js`, data tag `pairlock-btc-paper`). The triangle
strategy has no real-money switch.

**Bottom line**
- **Accounting is verified.** Matched pairs, the unmatched remainder, fees, slippage, mark-to-market and settlement are
  computed from fills only. 49 deterministic tests cover them, and every replayed market reconciles with its cash flows
  to 0.000000. Pending orders are never inventory, and a negative-net or partially matched pair can never be
  `PAIR_LOCKED`.
- **Edge A (the directional entry) is small and not proven.** Out of sample the gated entry made +0.14 per traded market
  (t < 1). On TRAIN it was about 0.
- **Edge B (hedging and pair locking) does not exist at our latency and costs.**
  - Buying the opposite token after a confirmed reversal is worth −0.28 / −0.29 / −0.46 USDC per evaluation at
    +1 s / +10 s / settlement, compared with not hedging.
  - Locking a pair after a favourable move is worth −0.17 / −0.18 / −0.25.
  - Every pair-cost threshold from 0.92 to 0.99 loses.
- **$1 per market is not achievable on this evidence.** The strongest defensible figure is C at +0.19 per traded market
  on the untouched TEST + FORWARD data (206 markets, t < 1). Its edge comes from the entry, not the hedge.

## B. Strategy rules (as running)

1. **Trigger.** The 1 s composite-spot shock: ≥ 2.5 σ on Binance, Bybit and Coinbase, with the 3 s move agreeing.
2. **Entry (Edge A).**
   - Settlement EV = P(side wins) − VWAP − fee − slippage − adverse selection must be ≥ **+0.02**.
     - P comes from a logistic model fitted on TRAIN.
     - The VWAP is the order's 10 shares walked through the live ask levels, assuming faster traders take 80 % of each
       level.
     - Slippage (0.77 ¢) and adverse selection (1.65 ¢) were measured on TRAIN.
   - P(the token's bid in 10 s ≥ the entry ask) must be ≥ **0.55**.
   - FOK at the deepest level used + 3 ¢.
   - Price band 0.20–0.96, ≥ 60 s left, at most 2 cycles per market.
   - A new cycle starts only when the previous one is closed: locked, settled, or fully matched with nothing pending.
3. **Adverse move.** The heavy side's bid is ≥ 3 ¢ under its average cost.
4. **Reversal.** An equal-weight score of 8 standardised components (token velocity 1 s and 3 s, token acceleration,
   spot momentum 1 s and 3 s, book imbalance, Polymarket depth flow, exchange agreement) must stay ≥ **1.5** for
   **400 ms**.
5. **Hedge evaluation.** It runs only on a confirmed reversal, or when a qualifying lock is available.
   - Candidates are 0 / 10 / … / 100 % of the **unmatched** quantity. Each is priced at the live VWAP, under every hard
     risk limit.
   - It computes for each: new quantities, matched and unmatched, total cost, pair cost and net edge, locked P&L,
     UP/DOWN resolution P&L, worst case, expected P&L, capital and fill probability.
   - The best hedge_EV is sent only if it is > **MIN_HEDGE_EV (+0.05 USDC)**. Otherwise the outcome is `NO_HEDGE`,
     logged as HEDGE_REJECTED, with a 5 s re-evaluation interval.
6. **Pair lock.** `PAIR_LOCKED` needs all of these:
   - matched quantity > 0, and UP == DOWN;
   - nothing pending;
   - net pair edge ≥ **0.05** (pair cost ≤ 0.95 including fees);
   - locked P&L ≥ **0.05 USDC**.

   Anything less stays `PAIR_FORMED`.
7. **Close and settlement.** At the close, `SETTLEMENT_PENDING`. On the venue outcome, `SETTLED`, and the engine's P&L
   is reconciled with the bot's. A difference over 0.01 halts new entries.

States: NO_POSITION → INITIAL_ENTRY_CANDIDATE → INITIAL_ENTRY → MONITORING ⇄ ADVERSE_MOVE → HEDGE_EVALUATION →
NO_HEDGE | PARTIAL_HEDGE | FULL_HEDGE → PAIR_FORMED → PAIR_LOCKED → SETTLEMENT_PENDING → SETTLED. Any transition
not in `TRANSITIONS` raises `InvalidTransition`.

## C. The mathematical model

- **Accounting** (per cycle, from fills):
  - `up_total = up_gross + up_fees` (same for DOWN); `total_cost = up_total + down_total`.
  - `up_avg = up_gross / up_qty`.
  - `matched = min(up, down)`; `unmatched_up = max(up − down, 0)`.
  - `matched_pair_cost = (up_avg + down_avg)·matched + fees·(matched/qty)` per side.
  - `pair_edge = 1 − up_avg − down_avg`; `net_pair_edge = pair_edge − allocated fees per pair`.
  - `locked_pair_pnl = matched − matched_pair_cost`.
  - `UP-resolution P&L = up_qty − total_cost`; `DOWN-resolution P&L = down_qty − total_cost`.
  - `worst case = min(both) = matched − total_cost`, tested on 300 random books.
  - `MTM = up_qty·up_bid + down_qty·down_bid − total_cost` (executable bids).
  - `expected = p_up·up_qty + (1 − p_up)·down_qty − total_cost`.
  - **Slippage** (fill − expected price) is measured and shown, but it is already inside the price paid, so it is
    not added to the cash cost a second time. Adding it would break the cash reconciliation (test 10).
- **Entry EV** per share: `P(side) − VWAP − fee(VWAP) − slip − adverse`.
- **Hedge EV**: `P(fill) · (E[P&L after] − E[P&L without]) = P(fill) · q · (P(B) − VWAP_B − fee_B)`.
  - The sunk loss on A never enters it.
  - Sizes are fractions of the unmatched quantity, never of the loss.
  - Test: two positions identical except the size of the loss get the same decision. A larger loss can only push a
    hedge past MAX_PAIR_COST, which blocks it; it never enlarges it.
- **Optimal hedge quantity**: the argmax of hedge_EV over the feasible candidates. Because hedge_EV is linear in q, a
  positive B edge gives the full feasible size and a negative one gives no hedge. A partial hedge happens only when depth
  or limits cut the full size.

## D. Execution model and latency

**Bot measurements** (forensics on 18 orders, plus the latency report):

| stage | p50 |
|---|---|
| exchange event → received | 113 ms (Coinbase 72, Bybit 118, Binance 153) |
| received → signal | 11 ms |
| signal → decision → sent | 0 ms |
| Polymarket book venue time → received | 35 ms |
| sent → venue fill | 159 ms |
| order round trip | 224 ms p50, 694 ms p95 |

Coinbase quotes arrive first; Binance is slowest here.

**The replay's execution:**
- Arrival = decision + 143 ms.
- The size is walked through the ask levels at arrival: touch, +1 ¢, +2 ¢, a third of the near-touch depth each, with
  80 % taken by faster traders.
- The book 1 s later must still fill the size at or under the limit.
- The fill is charged the worse VWAP of the two books; per-level taker fee.
- A failed FOK leaves nothing.

**TRAIN fill rates:** entries 71 %, hedges 87 %. The replay's fill model reproduces the paper venue's results (test).

## G. Failure analysis

- **Losing entries.** The worst market is −8.8 to −9.9 (a 10-share first leg near 0.85–0.95 that lost). The
  5th-percentile market is about −7.5. Entries with 60–120 s left are few and poor.
- **Bad reversals.** The counterfactuals above: a confirmed reversal does not make the opposite token cheap. Its ask is
  about 1 − bid_A, so the pair costs 1 + the loss.
- **Expensive hedges.** B's pairs average 1.14 per pair: −298 on matched pairs over held-out data.
- **Incomplete hedges.** C: 31–80 % of a period's few hedge orders (n 5–16) were killed. B: 10–22 %. Killed hedges leave
  the exposure as it was; they are logged, never re-placed as resting orders.
- **Liquidity.** Low-liquidity windows lose for every strategy (−0.40 to −0.72 per market).
- **Latency.** 29 % of TRAIN entries were killed (the ask left the limit within the survival window). The fills that
  remain carry 1.65 ¢ of adverse selection.
- **Drawdowns.** On TRAIN: A −96, C −90, B −58 (hedging lowers drawdown and costs mean), baseline −120.
