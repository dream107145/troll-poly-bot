# FAK (fill-and-kill) entries (2026-09-29)

**Answer.** Entries can now be fill-and-kill (`TPB_SPOT_LAG_ORDER_TYPE=FAK`, default `FOK`): the order takes
whatever the book holds under the limit when it lands, up to the target size, and cancels the rest. A paper bot
(`tpb-spotlag-paper-fak`) runs it with the live bot's settings otherwise; the real-money bot is unchanged.

## Why, and how much to expect

- **The fast traders appear to use FAK.** In the fill sizes of x-MoneyForWhiskas's 26,469 taker matches, 39%
  are exactly 50 shares, 57% are smaller (mostly makers' round sizes), and none are larger than 100. That is a
  50-share cap filled with whatever was there, which is FAK's fingerprint.
- **For us the replay says about +10% fills.** On the recorded books, 80% of our FOK kills happen because the
  price has moved above the limit when the order lands; FAK gets nothing there either. The other ~2% of kills
  find the price still in range but only 5–9 shares, and FAK fills those.

## What changed (tests in `test_spot_lag.py`, `test_polymarket_exchange.py`, `test_take_profit.py`)

| where | change |
|---|---|
| `SpotLagConfig.order_type` | `FOK` (default) or `FAK`; FAK entries are `TimeInForce.IOC` orders of the full target size, sent when at least the venue minimum (5 shares) shows under the cap; the edge is priced on the visible shares |
| live bot, last look before POST | a FOK needs its whole size under the cap; a FAK needs only the venue minimum |
| live adapter | IOC entries are signed as `order_type="FAK"`; a partial reply is booked as a partial fill (state `PARTIAL`); a FAK "found nothing" reply (`no orders found to match …`, `… FAK orders are partially filled or killed`, code `fak_not_filled`) is a kill, never an API error, so it cannot build toward the 5-error halt |
| exits | stay FOK; a position below the venue minimum (a 1–4-share partial, or rounding dust) is held to settlement instead of retrying a sell the venue will refuse (`EXIT_BELOW_MIN_SIZE`) |
| paper venue | already filled IOC orders partially; unchanged |

## Risks

- A 1–4-share fill (about 0.1% of orders in the replay) cannot be sold; it rides to settlement with no take-profit
  or stop-loss.
- The venue's exact wording for an empty FAK was not seen live; the adapter recognises the SDK codes and the
  known phrasings. An unrecognised reply would count as an API error, and five in a row halt the bot: watch
  the first live FAK kills before relying on it.

## Judging the paper test

Compare `tpb-spotlag-paper-fak` with the live bot over the same hours: fills per order, the share of partial
fills and their size, and whether the recorded books agree with its fills (the audit in
`docs/spot_lag_paper_history_2026-09-25.md`). Its dashboard P&L alone is not evidence.
