# BTC momentum → reversal pairs, paper-tested in `tpb-fastclip-paper` (2026-10-01)

**Answer.** Implemented as switches on the existing `btc_trend_reversal_hedge` engine and replayed
chronologically on 1,465 BTC windows. The locked configuration (chosen on TRAIN + VALIDATION only) makes
**+$40.9 on train, +$4.4 on validation, −$19.5 on TEST and −$71.6 on FORWARD** (1,081 windows, t = −1.8).
Only one first leg in five finds a hedge under the cap; the other four hold a directional 5-share
position to settlement and lose it 63 % of the time. **No statistically credible edge.** The paper app
runs it anyway, as asked, to measure real paper fills; it must not go near real money.

## What was built (focused; no refactor)

| file | change |
|---|---|
| `strategy/btc_hedge.py` | `max_pairs_per_market`, `min_seconds_between_legs`, `min_pair_edge` (pair cap = min(max_pair_cost, 1 − edge)); a completed pair rotates into the next first leg (`_next_pair`, `PAIR_DONE` log); settlement from per-side totals so pairs of either orientation add up; `pairs`, `pair_log` in MARKET_END |
| `scripts/research_btc_pairs.py` | the chronological replay: signal-only first leg, grid trend_z × confirm_s × max_pair_cost × leg gap on TRAIN+VALIDATION, lock `data/replay/btc_pairs_locked.json`, TEST/FORWARD untouched, per-window table, hour breakdown |
| `ecosystem.fastclip.config.js` | `tpb-fastclip-paper` now runs the pairs strategy (data tag `btcpairs-paper`); the original clip app is kept as `tpb-fastclip-paper-clips`, not started |
| `tests/test_btc_hedge.py` | +3 tests (second pair after the gap, hedge waits for gap and edge, one pair by default); suite 1,056 |

Every parameter in the brief is configurable (`TPB_BTC_HEDGE_*` / dashboard): momentum `trend_z`, reversal
`reversal_confirmation_seconds` (same σ), `min_seconds_between_legs`, `max_pair_cost`, `min_pair_edge`,
`initial_entry_size`, `max_pairs_per_market`. Execution: the live book with the 60 ms / 1 s age gates, the
measured latency model, taker fees, touch/FAK sizing (`initial_fak_after`), all existing risk limits.
Every signal, order, fill, reversal, HEDGE_CHECK (pair cost, decision), rejection reason and MARKET_END
P&L is logged by the engine and the decision journal.

The loss is the 80 % of first legs that never get a hedge under the cap (the reversal comes with the
opposite token already repriced) and hold a 0.34 token that settles at 0 in 63 % of windows. By hour, the
forward loss is spread over the day (worst 00–01 UTC −$39, best 12 UTC +$14); no window class rescues it.

## Verdict

NOT a candidate for money. Paper app `tpb-fastclip-paper` (tag `btcpairs-paper`) is running for the plumbing
and fill measurement; `__main__.check_btc_hedge` refuses micro-live, live and shadow unconditionally.
