# Fast feed: detecting the start of a spot move at tick resolution (2026-09-29)

**Answer.** The feeds were already direct WebSocket, in-memory and non-blocking. What was slow was
the *signal*: a 1 s return in sigma, measured on a spot history sampled every 200 ms. The fast feed
watches every raw exchange quote per asset, fires when the return over 50 / 100 / 250 / 500 ms is
abnormal *for that horizon* (same k, sqrt-scaled, plus a spread floor), and asks the strategy to
evaluate at once; the strategy's own gates decide. On 135 min of tick recordings it detects the same
moves **119 ms earlier** overall (117–197 ms on BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and DOGE; 6 on BNB) and fills 34–39 %
of its orders against the venue book versus 19 % for the current rule. P&L is not distinguishable from
the current rule on this sample (9–18 fills per rule). It is off by default and not on real money; a
paper app is defined.

## Reading it honestly

- **Timing**: the 250 ms rule sees the same moves 117–197 ms earlier on BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and DOGE,
  and not on BNB (its Binance quotes lead everything else by so much that the 1 s rule is as early);
  the 500 ms rule is no earlier than the 1 s rule.
- **Fills**: 42 of 119 orders (35 %) across the three fast variants versus 10 of 52 (19 %) for the
  current rule. That is the one difference larger than noise (about p = 0.03).
- **P&L**: 9–18 fills per rule; a single fill is ±5 USDC. No rule separates from the current one. Rule E,
  which fires *later* than A, has as many fills and profit as any — a hint that a burst-confirmed move
  fills as well as an early one, not evidence.
- **The lead composite (rule B, live now)**: 2.6x the signals, 25 fills, −0.03 per fill in this replay
  against +2.01 for the median rule. Small sample, but it is the setting real money runs on since 17:58:
  watch `data/trade_forensics.jsonl` fills and P&L per order over the next hours.
- **Thresholds were not tuned**: k is the strategy's own, the spread floor is 1.5x the quoting
  exchange's spread, the horizons are the brief's.

## Remaining bottleneck

Not detection. Of the current rule's 890 signals, 341 fail the edge gate and 325 the price band before
any order; of the 52 orders, 42 are killed because the venue's ask is above the limit 135 ms later.
The venue's own 216 ms round trip and the token repricing inside it are the wall; live today the wall
took every one of 67 orders.
